What is a dispute?
Use disputes when something goes wrong after the owner accepted your offer and the contribution is in the active send window (status “pending”). Examples: the post office refuses the shipment, the QR/slip can’t be used, wrong drop-off details, or you need staff help to unblock the workflow.
